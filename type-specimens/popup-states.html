Specimen · Popup states · Color directions (archive)

The popup is a state machine with three ignitions: detection (confident / ambiguous / empty), extraction (reading / capturing), and resolution (success / warning / error / fired). Each variant below renders at true 400 px Chrome MV3 width against the v2 brutalist palette, to stress-test layout, hierarchy, and edge cases before build.

DetectionClient-side, free. Decides which of the three open-states to show before any Claude call.
ExtractionClaude call in flight. Reading page or capturing video frames. Fire is disabled until a .md is on hand.
ResolutionTerminal state — filled card, low-confidence flag, retryable error, or a fired receipt.
Flame budgetOne accent family. Fire button, ✶ glyph, extraction badge, urgent states — nothing else chromatic.

Gochujang-Glazed Pork Belly

slow-rendered, sharply-sauced, served over rice with a soft egg.

SERVES 4 HANDS-ON 25m TOTAL 2h 10m

Ingredients

  • 800 gpork belly, skin-on
  • 3 tbspgochujang paste
  • 2 tbsphoney or maltose
  • 1½ tbspsoy sauce (light)
  • 4 clovesgarlic, crushed
  • 1 thumbginger, julienned
  • —spring onion & sesame to finish

Method

  1. Score the pork belly skin in a crosshatch, just through the fat. Salt heavily, uncovered in the fridge overnight — this is non-negotiable.
  2. Sear skin-down in a dry cast-iron until the skin blisters and shatters when tapped. Drain most of the fat.
  3. Whisk gochujang, honey, soy, garlic and ginger with 100 ml water. Pour around (not over) the pork. Cover, oven 160 °C for 90 minutes.
  4. Uncover, turn skin-up, ladle sauce over every few minutes for the final 15 minutes at 200 °C until lacquered.

Cacio e Pepe, On Ice

the cold-noodle heresy that works.

SERVES 2 HANDS-ON 15m TOTAL 15m

Ingredients

  • 200 gspaghetti
  • 80 gpecorino romano, microplaned
  • 2 tspblack pepper, freshly cracked
  • —ice water, for shock

Method

  1. Cook pasta 1 min past al dente in heavily salted water.
  2. Shock in ice water, drain.
  3. Toss with pecorino, pepper, 1 tbsp pasta water. Serve immediately.

State grammar — invariants

Rules observed across every variant above.

Source lineAlways visible, even when empty. It's the promise the card is about to keep or decline.
Chyron placementUnder the source line, above the content. Kitchen-ticket register — never floats over the card.
Fire semanticsOnly enabled when a usable .md exists. Swapped for Retry / Paste URL when no artifact is in reach.
Esc contractCancels the current ignition cleanly. During extraction, aborts the Claude call. During Fired, cancels auto-close.
Callout shapeFull 1px border box (not side-stripe). Stamp register, not alert register. Border color carries severity.
Flame budgetFire · ✶ · extraction badge (ember) · urgent states. Never chromatic decoration.
