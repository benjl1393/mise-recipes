Gochujang-Glazed Pork Belly

slow-rendered, sharply-sauced, served over rice with a soft egg.

SERVES 4 HANDS-ON 25m TOTAL 2h 10m

Ingredients

  • 800 gpork belly, skin-on
  • 3 tbspgochujang paste
  • 2 tbsphoney or maltose

Method

  1. Score the skin in a crosshatch, salt heavily, uncovered overnight.
  2. Sear skin-down in dry cast-iron until the skin shatters when tapped.
