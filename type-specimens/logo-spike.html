DecisionTreat full (with rail) as the brand mark for hero/landing/lockup, and the rod-less fired ticket as the product icon — with a stripped minimal reduction for ≤16px. One family, three levels of detail by size.
ConsideredForcing one geometry at every size. The rail just doesn't read at 16 — pretending it does would ship a blob.
TensionDoes the rod-less ticket still say Mise without the rail, or does it drift toward a generic receipt? And does the minimal 16px reduction hold its kitchen character, or is it just "doc + red line"?
