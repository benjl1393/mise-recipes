Gochujang-Glazed Pork Belly

slow-rendered, sharply-sauced, served over rice with a soft egg.

SERVES 4 HANDS-ON 25m TOTAL 2h 10m

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 800 gpork belly, skin-on
  • 3 tbspgochujang paste
  • 2 tbsphoney or maltose
  • 1½ tbspsoy sauce (light)
  • 4 clovesgarlic, crushed
  • 1 thumbginger, julienned

Method

Method

  1. Score the pork belly skin in a crosshatch, just through the fat. Salt heavily, uncovered in the fridge overnight.
  2. Sear skin-down in a dry cast-iron until the skin blisters and shatters when tapped.
  3. Whisk gochujang, honey, soy, garlic and ginger with 100 ml water. Pour around the pork. Cover, oven 160 °C for 90 minutes.
  4. Uncover, turn skin-up, ladle sauce over for the final 15 minutes at 200 °C until lacquered.
